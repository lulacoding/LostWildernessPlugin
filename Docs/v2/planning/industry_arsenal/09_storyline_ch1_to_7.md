# Lost Wilderness: The 7-Chapter Storyline

## Chapter 1: Restoring Cosmic Balance

**The First Boot & Arrival:**
- *Cutscene 1 (Pre-Lobby):* The player connects for the very first time. An unskippable, tile-based video cutscene plays full-screen before they even see the Lobby. (Content TBD).
- *The Awakening:* The player spawns in the Lobby, verifies their account, and chooses their initial traits.
- *Cutscene 2 (Survival Entry):* Stepping through the Gateway portal into the Survival world triggers a second cinematic, establishing their physical arrival on Earth. (Content TBD).

**The First Anomaly:**
The player begins standard survival — punching trees, mining, and gearing up. Nothing appears out of the ordinary. The first true divergence happens when they reach the End dimension. They do not face the Ender Dragon. They face **Mother Ender**. Defeating her is the first major narrative milestone.

**The First Wither & Professor Kraft:**
Returning to the Overworld, the player gathers wither skulls and summons their first Wither. Upon defeating it, a random world encounter triggers: a roaming NPC named **Professor Kraft** appears. He leaves them with a cryptic directive: *"Experiment with crying obsidian. When you have bridged the dimensions, we will speak again."*

**The Corrupt Portal (Dimensional Bridge):**
The player builds an Amplified Portal (Crying Obsidian frame ignited with flint and steel). Stepping through transports them to the **Amplified World** — a parallel Earth with impossibly extreme vertical terrain (Y=320).

**The Amplified Errand & Side Quests:**
Professor Kraft intercepts them in the Amplified World. He provides coordinates to his nearest lab and tasks them with retrieving RNG-selected items from the Amplified terrain. Between this information drop and the next, Kraft offers several **Side Quests** to pace the progression.

**Father of End:**
After delivering the items and completing side quests, Kraft directs the player to the **Amplified End** to slay the counterpart to Mother Ender: the **Father of End**.

*(End of Chapter 1)*

## Chapter 2: Taking on the Withering Council

**The Wither Hunt & Larry the Assistant:**
Professor Kraft tasks the player with slaying 5 more Withers. Between each Wither, there are side quests from Kraft. He introduces his lab assistant, **Larry** — who only cares about running fetch quests. Larry hands the player maps to specific custom biomes and asks them to bring back custom items found there. His only function is to get players exploring the world.

**Breaching the Roof:**
After the 5th Wither, Kraft's curiosity turns to the Nether ceiling. He tasks the player with physically breaking through the Bedrock to scout the Nether Roof (Y=128). The player does it, scouts the roof, and returns to the lab to report. Larry casually suggests: *"Why not try spawning a Wither up there?"* Kraft formally agrees and tasks the player to go back and summon one, then report back.

**The First Devoider:**
The Wither on the Normal Nether Roof mutates into **The Devoider**. Player defeats it, returns to Kraft. Kraft is excited by the enhanced Nether Star drops — useful for his new research. He asks the player to farm more.

**The Grind to El Diablo:**
The player must summon and defeat 6 more Devoiders. During this grind, more side quests are offered and potential new characters may be introduced.
As the final (6th) Devoider dies, it taunts the player: *"You will never guess where El Diablo is hiding."*

**The Portal to El Diablo's Lair:**
Professor Kraft, connecting the clues, recommends attempting a Wither summon on the **Amplified Nether Roof**. When the player does this, no Devoider spawns. Instead, the ritual tears open a personalized, instanced **portal to El Diablo's Lair** — accessible only to that solo player or their specific party.

**El Diablo's Lair:**
Inside there are puzzles to solve. The environment is filled with Wither Roses. At the end, the player encounters El Diablo. Before combat begins, he offers a choice:
- **Evil Path:** Accept the Wither Rose and join El Diablo. He immediately takes the player to the **Moon** — beginning the Evil campaign.
- **Good Path:** Refuse the Wither Rose and fight him to the death.

*(Good Path):*
**The Heavens Open:**
Gabriel descends after El Diablo's defeat: *"I bring great news. The Lord wishes to speak with you."*

*(End of Chapter 2)*

## Chapter 3: Ascending the Firmament

**The Aether Portal:**
The player must build a **Glowstone** portal frame and ignite it with a **Water Bucket**. This transports them to the start of the heavenly ascent.

**The Hike to Heaven (Aquaria to The Lord):**
There are no boss fights on this journey — it is a long, loot-filled pilgrimage through several layers:
1. **Aquaria** — A fully submerged world. Players can breathe underwater. They navigate an underwater empire filled with loot.
2. **Cascade Tower** — In the center of Aquaria. Players climb to its peak.
3. **The Rainbow Path** — At the top of Cascade Tower, the Rainbow Path begins: a high-speed, vertical ice-boat highway made of rainbow blocks that winds up through the clouds.
4. **Heaven's Tower** — A 3,000-block-tall tower containing only a spiral staircase.

**The Holy Inquisition:**
At the top of Heaven's Tower, **Gabriel** administers the **Holy Questionnaire** to each player individually. Passing the test opens the final cloud stairs to **The Lord's Plateau**.

*(End of Chapter 3)*

## Chapter 4: Personality-Skills, Elemental and Chakra Quests

**The Lord's Plateau & The Descent:**
Having passed Gabriel's inquisition, the player walks the cloud stairs to the summit of the heavens: **The Lord's Plateau**. Here, they meet **The Lord** in a serene, untroubled dimension. The Lord reveals that the universe is vast, and the player is not yet strong enough to face the cosmic threats stirring beyond the sky. They must master the physical, elemental, and spiritual planes before the stars open to them.
To return to Earth and begin these trials, the player descends via the **Red Slide** — a massive, physical red ice-boat highway that drops them from the heights of Heaven all the way back down to the Overworld.

**The Evil Mirror (The Moon):**
Evil players are already on the Moon, working through their dark mirror requirements at El Diablo's Castle, guided by the Witch of the Night Sky and the 8 Demon Allies (Malphas, Vraxen, Ose, Kolveth, Haagenti, Drauzim, Vaelith, Zorrik).

**Trial 1 — Mastery of Self (Both Paths):**
- Max out Personality Traits and AuraSkills (universal).
- Prestige every AuraSkills skill once — Prestige 1 of 10 (universal, handled entirely by AuraSkills plugin).
- *Good:* Ascend to **Angel Honor (+4,000)** — 3rd highest rank.
- *Evil:* Descend to **Condemned Honor (-2,500)** — 3rd lowest rank. Branded **Demon** at the Void Altar.

**Trial 2 — The 5 Elemental Temples (Both Paths):**
Both Good and Evil players visit the same 5 Elemental Temples. Evil players travel via Demon Core.
1. **Temple of Earth** — Normal Overworld dungeon crawl
2. **Temple of Air** — Amplified Overworld dungeon crawl
3. **Temple of Fire** — Volcano Biome in the Amplified World
4. **Temple of Water** — Submerged in the Normal Ocean
5. **Temple of Aether** — Found in Aquaria. Populated by Angel NPCs. Ends with an Archangel quiz.
- *Good:* Complete all internal trials to earn elemental powers.
- *Evil:* Visit as silent observers. Absorb powers without disrupting the temples. Powers stay dormant until the Void Altar ritual.

**Trial 3 — The 7 Chakra Dimensions (Both Paths):**
Both paths access the same 6 dimensions. Good guided by Kraft's Monk. Evil guided by the 8 Demon Allies.

| Dimension | Chakra |
|---|---|
| Large Biome 1 | Root |
| Large Biome 1 Amplified | Sacral |
| Large Biome 2 | Solar Plexus |
| Large Biome 2 Amplified | Heart |
| Large Biome 3 | Throat |
| Large Biome 3 Amplified | Mind's Eye |
| The Lord / Void Altar | Crown (granted at completion) |

**The Crown Chakra — Activation:**
- *Good:* The Lord personally grants the Crown on the Plateau. All powers activate. Players at Angel ascend to **Archangel**.
- *Evil:* Void Altar ritual on the Moon. All powers activate. Condemned players are branded **Demon**.

**The Starport / Demon Core Unlocks:**
All trials complete. The Starport opens (Good) and the Demon Core fully unlocks (Evil). Only **Venus** is initially accessible.

*(End of Chapter 4)*
To return to Earth and begin these trials, the player uses the **Red Slide** — a massive, physical ice-boat highway that drops them from the heights of Heaven all the way back down to the Overworld.

**The Evil Mirror (The Moon):**
*Note: Because Evil players accepted the Wither Rose in Chapter 2, they bypass Heaven entirely. They are unholy and cannot enter Aquaria. Instead, El Diablo takes them directly to the Moon. They complete their Personality, Skills, Elemental, and Chakra trials (their "Evil Chapter 3") under the dark tutelage of El Diablo and the Witch of the Night Sky.*

**The First Trial: Prestige & Mastery:**
- Max out Personality Traits and AuraSkills.
- Reach **Prestige 1** in their profession.
- Reach **Angel** Honor tier (+2500).

**Trial 2 — The 5 Elemental Temples:**
Scattered across the world. Each ends in a dynamically-scaled boss fight:
1. Temple of Fire
2. Temple of Water
3. Temple of Earth
4. Temple of Air
5. Temple of Aether

**Trial 3 — The Chakra Parallel Worlds:**
Three distinct **Large Biome Amplified** dimensions (combining massive biome sprawling with Y=320 extreme vertical terrain). Players unlock **Chakra Anchors** within each, permanently expanding mana capacity and unlocking higher-tier AuraSkills abilities.

*(Evil Pathway Note: During "Evil Chapter 3" on the Moon, players must descend their Honor to the **Condemned** tier (-2500) to prove their loyalty to El Diablo, mirroring the Angel requirement for Good players).*

**The Starport Unlocks:**
All three trials complete. The Lord grants access to further cloud stairs leading to the **Starport** — a celestial hub containing portals to all Solar System planets. Only **Venus** is initially active.

*(End of Chapter 4)*

## Chapter 5: Ascending the Stars

**The Starport Unlocks:**
All three trials complete. The Lord grants access to further cloud stairs leading to the **Starport** — a celestial hub containing portals to all Solar System planets. Only **Venus** is initially active.

**Venus (The Baptism of Fire):**
The first space trial. Fire immunity (earned from maxed Personality/Skills) lets the player survive the extreme surface temperature. The player must survive the surface, gear up, and descend into the planet's core.

**The Cross-Path Core Bosses (Good vs. Evil):**
The Core Bosses of Venus and Mercury introduce a groundbreaking PvPvE mechanic. 
- *The Convergence:* While Good players travel here via The Lord's Starport, Evil players travel here using a **Demon Core** (a portable, single-use portal generator that destroys itself upon entry and is bound exclusively to its "master" player's UUID).
- *The Battle:* The Core boss fight is an instanced, synchronized matchmaking event. A Good party (or solo player) and an Evil party (or solo player) are thrown into the same boss arena simultaneously.
- *The Condition:* They must fight the boss *and* each other. To progress the story, a party must land the final blows or secure the victory condition against both the boss and the opposing alignment's party. If the Good party wins, the Evil party fails the instance and must re-queue to try again against a new Good party.

**Mercury:**
Venus progression naturally leads to Mercury. The player must repeat the survival and gearing process, culminating in another high-stakes PvPvE Core Boss fight against the opposing alignment.

**The Moon Port Unlocks:**
Having survived both planets, defeated the Core Bosses, and secured the Venus and Mercury exclusive skills/enchantments, the player returns to The Lord. He unlocks the Moon Port.

*(End of Chapter 5)*

---

## Chapter 6: The Lunar Anomaly & The Solar System

**The Moon:**
The true source of the Blood Eclipses is revealed here. The player fights **The Witch of the Night Sky** alongside a returning **El Diablo** in a massive lunar sky arena. This is a full PvPvE convergence — Good (Starport) and Evil (Demon Core) players fight each other and both bosses simultaneously. Defeating them ends the dark magic causing the Eclipses.

**Mars:**
- *Environment:* Zero oxygen. Bases and farms must be fully enclosed with functional airlocks and oxygen generators powered by Diesel (shipped from Earth or refined locally from Martian oil wells). Constant dust storms and heatwaves.
- *Politics:* Populated by Martian civilizations bound by the Honor system. Some are friendly; some are hostile.
- *Core Boss:* Stop a Rogue Martian tribe attempting to detonate the planet's core by igniting the deep Martian oil reserves.

**Jupiter:**
- *Environment:* A gas giant with End-like floating island generation. No civilizations, only ruins with loot. Rich in industrial metals (Iron, Coal, Copper, Aluminum, Lead). Constant violent storm events.
- *The Mantle Clash:* Good and Evil players fight each other in the Mantle before the Core. Only the victors proceed.
- *Core Boss (Good):* A lost Orion space-jet fleet that crashed centuries ago and survived on the gas planet's central islands.

**Saturn:**
- *Rings:* Dense asteroid field rich in precious gems (Diamonds, Emeralds, Jades, Rubies).
- *Core Boss:* **El Diablo + 2 buffed Devoiders**, fought alongside a PvPvE clash with Evil players.

**Uranus:**
- *Environment:* Three massive, distinct biomes for the simple blue alien societies:
  1. *Crystalline Resonance:* Translucent cyan spires in a frozen ammonia sea. The crystals hum; striking them produces musical notes. Hazard: Sonic Storms that damage uninsulated players.
  2. *Bioluminescent Neon Tundra:* Eternal twilight covered in glowing moss and stalk plants. Hazard: Shadow Pockets (pitch-black zones spawning light-fearing predators).
  3. *Ammonia Oceans:* Vast, toxic oceans with floating pumice islands and rickety rope bridges. Hazard: Random toxic geyser eruptions.
- *Mantle Clash:* Good vs. Evil PvP bottleneck before the Core.
- *Core Boss:* An alien **Rockstar Band** — a rhythm game mechanic fight.

**Neptune:**
- *Environment:* A frozen icy planet civilized by Yeti-like aliens.
- *Core Boss:* A massive **Yeti boss** fight with PvPvE against Evil players.

**Pluto:**
- *Environment:* Empty, flat, grey planet. Incredibly abundant in raw ores. Civilized by tiny grey "**Plutonians**."
- *Core Boss:* Another **Rock Band** rhythm game encounter. Good and Evil compete against each other via points in a dance battle mechanic.

**The Edge of Sol:**
After Pluto, players are transported to **The Edge of Sol** — a massive transparent glass platform suspended at the absolute boundary of the Solar System.
- *The Boss:* **El Diablo, Destroyer of Sol** — a visually distinct and significantly more powerful form than any previous encounter. A full PvPvE convergence where Good and Evil fight each other and El Diablo simultaneously.

*(End of Chapter 6)*

## Chapter 7: The Andromeda Galaxy (Zeratari System)

**The Faint Signal:**
After Pluto, The Lord reveals a faint signature of El Diablo's corruption in the Andromeda Galaxy. The **Galaxy Port** opens beyond the Starport. First portal active: **Myrikane** (the dark mirror of Earth).

**NPC Alliance:**
- Solo players gain 1 NPC Ally.
- 8-player party gains 8 NPC Allies (total 16-unit skirmishes).

**The Andromeda Conquest:**
1. Velkorn (Scorched city-sprawl. Brute NPC Empires.)
2. Zeruun (Acid bubble-cities. Brute NPC Empires.)
3. Xarneth (Endless desert.)
4. Myrikane (Dark Earth mirror. Entry point. Good-war ally.)
5. Kyrothis (Amplified frozen peaks. Rebel faction fights FOR Orion.)
6. Tarnaxis (Underground canyon societies. Too hot on surface. Evil clans fight for Orion.)
7. Qirellon (Dense pine forests. Skinny Hivemind aliens. Orion's allies.)
8. Nytharix (Cyberpunk Republic. Good alignment. Triggering the Galactic War when Earth's clan joins.)
9. **Orion** (Authoritarian Cyberpunk Death Star. Allied with El Diablo.)

**The Destruction of Orion:**
The final mission: sabotage the planetary core. Orion is destroyed. Its ruins remain permanently on the server as a scavengeable post-apocalyptic wasteland.

**Return to the Lord's Plateau** — where the final siege begins.

*(End of Chapter 7)*

## Chapter 8: The Reality Checkerboard

**The Siege of Heaven:**
Fringe Orion remnants led by El Diablo breach The Lord's Plateau.

**The Reality Checkerboard:**
The Lord teleports everyone into a cosmic chess board arena.
- 8 Good players vs. 8 Bad players (dynamically scaled with NPCs if needed).
- Special pieces: major story characters. Pawns: human players, Professor Kraft, Professor Krystal (Good side) vs. Orion Grunts (Evil side).
- *The Win Condition:* Good players must survive. Evil players must kill all 8 Good players. 
- *The Divine Intervention:* The outcome is fixed. Even if Evil players "win" by killing the Good players, The Lord always intervenes. The fight continues until at least 1 human, The Redeemer, The Lord, and El Diablo remain alive.
- The Lord summons continuous lightning across the board. Players are frozen, unable to move, watching The Lord cast El Diablo out of Minecraft permanently. Even victorious Evil players are forced to watch their master be banished.

*(End of Chapter 8)*

---

## The Epilogue: Ascension & Damnation (The Finale)

- **Good Path:** The Lord crowns the player **Archangel**. They receive celestial abilities and items.
- **Evil Path:** El Diablo's banishment formally registers the player to the Abyss as a **Demon**. They receive abyssal abilities and items.
- **The Ultimate Reward:** A cascade of custom achievements unlocks. BOTH Good and Evil players receive the exact same unique custom **Music Disc** to signify the end of the narrative.
- An endgame **Credits Cutscene** plays before returning the player to spawn.
- *100% Easter Egg:* All Vanilla advancements + all custom advancements completed = a second meme-y Easter Egg Music Disc.
- The player is now permanently immune to Void damage and begins the post-game sandbox as a demigod (or primeval terror).

*(End of Storyline)*
