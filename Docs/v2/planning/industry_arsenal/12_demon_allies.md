
## The 8 Demon Allies (Chapter 3 Evil — El Diablo's Castle)

Players who chose the Evil path receive 8 Demon Allies introduced at El Diablo's Castle on the Moon. In gameplay, only the number of Demons matching the player count will be active (1 solo player = 1 Demon, up to 8 player party = all 8 Demons).

---

### The Eight Demons

1. **Malphas — The Architect**
   - *Role:* Siege warfare expert. Builds fortresses, destroys enemy structures. Primary military strategist.
   - *Story Function:* Guides players through the `/siege` and military command suite alongside the Witch of the Night Sky.

2. **Vraxen — The Warlord**
   - *Role:* Brutish, front-line heavy hitter. The strongest fighter in the group.
   - *Story Function:* Combat NPC Ally in battle sequences. Provides muscle during the planetary campaigns.

3. **Ose — The Deceiver**
   - *Role:* Master of infiltration, espionage, and psychological warfare. Never straightforward.
   - *Story Function:* Guides Evil players through hacking, Decryption Arrays, and the Qirellon Iron Dome mission.

4. **Kolveth — The Chemist**
   - *Role:* Obsessed with corrosive agents, poisons, and volatile compounds.
   - *Story Function:* Primary guide for the Zeruun chemical heist mission.

5. **Haagenti — The Alchemist**
   - *Role:* Transmutes and transforms materials. A more refined, thoughtful version of Kolveth.
   - *Story Function:* Teaches Evil players advanced crafting and alien material synthesis during the planetary campaigns.

6. **Drauzim — The Ice Walker**
   - *Role:* Controls cold energy. Completely calm, calculating, almost meditative.
   - *Story Function:* Primary guide for the Kyrothis frozen planet missions.

7. **Vaelith — El Diablo's Enforcer**
   - *Role:* El Diablo's most personally loyal Demon. Enforces order among the other seven.
   - *Story Function:* Stands closest to El Diablo in narrative scenes. The most authoritative voice among the 8 outside of El Diablo himself.

8. **Zorrik — The Practical One**
   - *Role:* Sarcastic, pragmatic, completely uninterested in ideology. Just wants loot.
   - *Story Function:* Takes Larry's role. Provides exploration and scouting fetch quests throughout the Evil campaign.
