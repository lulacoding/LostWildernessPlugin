# Lost Wilderness: Honor & Alignment Scale

## The Full Honor Scale

The Honor Score is a dynamic sliding scale. Every player has a running score that changes based on their actions. The score determines the player's **dynamic title**, which updates as they cross thresholds.

### Story-Gated Tiers (Top & Bottom)

| Score | Title | Unlocked By |
|---|---|---|
| +50,000 | **God** | Reality Checkerboard completion (Chapter 8) |
| +25,000 | **Demigod** | Edge of Sol completion (Chapter 6) |

## Key Honor Mechanics

### The Forgiveness System (Good Path)
- Only available to **Condemned** players (-2,500).
- Cannot be triggered by command — only available from **The Redeemer** NPC during **Easter Sunday** on the in-game Calendar.
- **Prerequisite:** The player must have eaten ONLY fish (no other food) for the preceding Calendar day (Good Friday fast).
- **What Forgiveness wipes:**
  - Honor score → reset to 0
  - Inventory + Ender Chest → fully wiped
  - Personality Traits → wiped
  - AuraSkills → wiped
  - Custom class items → wiped
- **What survives Forgiveness:**
  - Age (in-game Calendar years accumulated)
  - **The player is "Born Again"** — a fresh soul, older in years, with visible history.
- **Cannot save Damned players (-4,000 or below).** Forgiveness is only possible at Condemned (-2,500). Fall further and there is no return.

### The Holy War Trigger
- If the server-wide Sin-O-Meter hits 90%+ disparity between Good and Evil players, the server itself declares a Holy War automatically.
- No command. No player approval. The world forces the war.

### Honor-Gated Private Chat Channels
- `/heavens` — Angel (+4,000) and Archangel (+5,000) only. The Good council.
- `/shadows` — Condemned (-2,500) and Demon (-5,000) only. The dark council.
- These channels are invisible to players outside the eligible tier.

## Honor Triggers by Alignment

### Good Player Triggers (Honor gain)
- Heal injured players
- Donate resources to new players
- Complete Celestial quests (temple runs, Kraft's scientific missions)
- Defend villages from raids without looting them
- Spare enemy players in PvP
- Fast on Good Friday
- Visit sacred structures and complete their trials

### Evil Player Triggers (Honor loss)
- Kill passive players unprovoked
- Poison water supplies
- Betray allies
- Commit war crimes during declared wars (no declaration, civilian casualties)
- Conduct Cyber War attacks
- Recruit for Orion or El Diablo during Zeratari campaign
- Perform dark Void Altar rituals

*Design principle: Both paths have equal depth. Good players never miss out on "fun" mechanics — they have a fully parallel suite of celestial abilities, channels, and story rewards.*
