# Lost Wilderness: The Master Storyline & World Progression
*Transcribed from developer notes — linear story order*

## Phase 1: Earth (The Starting Servers)
**The Four Dimensions of Earth:**
- Survival World (Normal)
- Amplified World
- Nether → (contains El Diablo's Lair)
- End

---

## Phase 2: After Defeating El Diablo — The Heavens Open
Defeating El Diablo unlocks access to the divine realm:

**Heaven:**
- Aquaria
- Heaven's Tower → Rainbow Path
- Lord's Plateau → Slide back to Earth
- The Starport (unlocks after Elemental content)

**The Elemental Temple (Portal Room Hub):**
- Fire Temple
- Water Temple
- Earth Temple
- Air Temple
- Aether Temple

---

## Phase 3: The Chakra Parallel Worlds (Large Biome Worlds)
*Potentially accessible as a Survival II alternative*

After completing the Elemental content, the Chakra Parallel Worlds unlock:
- Large Biome I (Normal) + Amplified variant — W, N, E dimensions
- Large Biome II (Normal) + Amplified variant — W, N, E dimensions
- Large Biome III (Normal) + Amplified variant — W, N, E dimensions

---

## Phase 4: The Solar System
*Unlocked after completing Diablo, Elemental, Personality, and Chakra content*

### The Solar System Planets (in progression order):

**Venus**
- Surface
- Mantle
- Core (Glaciers)

**Mercury**
- Surface
- Mantle
- Core

**Moon**
- Surface
- Mantle

**Mars**
- Surface / Mantle / Core
- After Orion Core Boss → Ultimate Showdown

**Jupiter**
- Surface / Mantle / Core

**Saturn**
- Rings (unique dimension)
- Surface / Mantle / Core

**Uranus**
- Rings + Surface / Mantle / Core

**Neptune**
- Surface / Mantle / Core

**Pluto**
- Surface / Mantle / Core
- *Edge of Sol* — the final boundary of the Solar System
