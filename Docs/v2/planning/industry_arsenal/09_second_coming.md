
## The Second Coming (The Redeemer's Return)
When the world has suffered enough, The Redeemer intervenes — not to forgive, but to end.

**The Trigger Conditions (ALL must be true simultaneously):**
- A Holy War is active AND has escalated to include Nuclear, World War, Cyber War, or Civil War tags
- The conflict has lasted **over 100 in-game Calendar years**
- The exact trigger point is **randomized** between 100 and 150 years — nobody knows the exact day

**The Warning System (7 Calendar Days Before):**
The CalendarService begins sending omens. Every morning subtitle reads:
> *"The Heavens grow restless. Something ancient stirs."*
Signs appear in the world: blood rain, spontaneous lightning on clear days, Zodiac constellations burning bright even during daylight hours.

**The Event:**
On the random trigger day, a massive divine beam of light strikes the center of the largest active warzone. Every player across all 34 dimensions sees a full-screen title:
> **Title: THE REDEEMER HAS RETURNED**
> **Subtitle: The wars are over. The world will be made new.**

**The Mechanics (Map Restoration):**
- All active war states are forcibly ended by the server. No surrenders, no treaties.
- A world regeneration event begins:
  - All nuclear fallout zones are purged and terrain restored.
  - Corrupted biome spread is reversed.
  - Destroyed cities remain as ruins (scars preserved permanently).
  - New biome features generate in previously barren areas.
- All players receive a Judgment based on their final Honor score:
  - *Good-aligned:* Divine blessing — large Honor boost, rare Celestial item drop.
  - *Evil-aligned:* Divine curse — Honor slashed 25%, temporary Wither effect.
  - *Neutral:* No effect.
- Discord Chronicle posts a full War Epitaph — complete history from Day 1 to The Redeemer's arrival.

**What is NOT Reset:**
- Player data, inventories, pets, Clan structures, territory claims, political hierarchies.
- Ruins of destroyed cities remain as permanent archaeological sites.
- Calendar continues from the same year.
- War record in the Discord Chronicle is never deleted.
