# Lost Wilderness: The Zeratari System (Andromeda)

## The Cosmic Mirror (The Andromeda Concept)
The Zeratari System is the Andromeda Galaxy's dark reflection of the Milky Way. It is a hostile, alien frontier where the standard rules of Minecraft survival are warped by Void corruption, advanced alien ecosystems, and cosmic scale. Each planet possesses distinct physical laws, unique environmental hazards, and custom alien flora/fauna.

*Note: The progression path starts at Myrikane (the Earth mirror), but the remaining planets can be encountered as the player carves their way toward the final world of Orion.*

### 1. Velkorn (The Scorched Citadel)
- **The Concept:** The Zeratari equivalent to Mercury, but far more extreme. Rather than a barren rock, the entire planet is a sprawling, heat-shielded alien ecumenopolis (a planet-wide city). The inhabitants are **Velkorans**, a massive, brute-force alien species.
- **Environment & Hazards:** Extreme surface temperatures that bypass standard fire resistance (requiring specialized cooling equipment or advanced spells). The terrain is dominated by towering, brutalist alien architecture, molten metal rivers, and labyrinthine industrial sectors.
- **Key Resources:** Hyper-dense alloys required for the strongest vehicle chassis (Tanks/Mechs) and heat-resistant components.
- **The Local Politics:** Velkorn is not a unified world. The NPC Velkorans are divided into warring Alliances and IGOs. Players can engage with these NPC factions — choosing to fund one side in a proxy war or attempting to conquer the city sprawls block-by-block. They are brutes, lacking the philosophical depth of Celestial magic, but their physical engineering and tactical AI are superior to humanity's.
- **The Mission:** Alien Mad Max. The player is deployed to Velkorn to gather endgame alloys and gear up their forces. However, their primary story objective is to stop a dominant Velkoran IGO that is on the verge of officially allying with the Orion Empire.
- **The Gameplay Loop:** Constant, brutal street-level urban warfare. The hostile Velkoran factions actively start street fights and ambushes against the player's squad in the cramped, molten-metal-filled city streets. This is where the 8-unit **NPC Allied Squad** becomes mandatory for survival, holding choke points and laying down suppressing fire while the player advances.
- **The Core Encounter:** The player must breach the heavily fortified capital of the Orion-aligned Velkoran IGO and assassinate its Warlord before the treaty with Orion can be signed.

### 2. Zeruun (The Acidic Empire)
- **The Concept:** A hyper-lethal mirror to Venus. The atmosphere is a dense, corrosive soup, yet the **Zeruunites** have built massive, enclosed bubble-cities and floating arcologies above the acidic cloud layer.
- **Environment & Hazards:** The open air rapidly degrades armor durability and inflicts a custom "Corrosion" debuff that damages health directly. Players must navigate between the safe-zones (the bubble cities) or use Tier 4 Hazmat vehicles (like sealed submarines designed for acid lakes) to traverse the planet.
- **Key Resources:** Corrosive isotopes and rare biochemical agents used by Master Doctors/Bio-Chemists to synthesize endgame combat stims and biological weapons.
- **The Local Politics:** The Zeruunites share the brutal, pragmatic intelligence of their Velkoran neighbors. They have their own sprawling Empires and strict border controls. The player and their NPC squad must either negotiate passage through their airspace or launch full-scale sieges against their floating fortresses to secure the planetary Core.

### 3. Xarneth (The Endless Dune)
- **The Concept:** A hyper-arid, endless desert world spanning the entire globe. Massive dunes and subterranean sandstone structures. 
- **Environment & Hazards:** Severe heat and dehydration mechanics. Constant sandstorms limit visibility to near-zero, forcing players to rely on fiber-optic radar systems to navigate between oases.
- **Key Resources:** Rare crystalline silicates required for advanced laser weaponry and 64-channel redstone microchips.
- **The Local Politics:** Nomadic, highly territorial scavenger factions control the deep desert trade routes.

### 4. Myrikane (The Dark Mirror)
- **The Concept:** A dark, corrupted mirror to Earth. The first planet players step foot on in the Andromeda galaxy.
- **Environment & Hazards:** Features mutated, twisted versions of Overworld biomes. Forests of jagged, crystalline trees; oceans of thick, slow-moving viscous liquid.
- **Key Resources:** The entry-level alien isotopes required to begin researching the Tier 4 Alien Tech tree.
- **The Local Politics:** Currently embroiled in a massive Zeratari-wide war alongside Nytharix.
- **The Core Encounter:** The establishment of the Forward Operating Base alongside the NPC Allies. Here, the player first meets **Professor Krystal** — the cosmic, Myrikane-native counterpart to Earth's Professor Kraft. She serves as the primary guide and mission control for the Good-aligned forces throughout the entire Andromeda campaign.

### 5. Kyrothis (The Frozen Zenith)
- **The Concept:** An Amplified-style planet of impossible, towering ice peaks and bottomless frozen ravines.
- **Environment & Hazards:** Absolute zero temperatures require top-tier thermal gear (or continuous fire magic/diesel heaters). Avalanches are a constant threat.
- **Key Resources:** Super-cooled gases essential for Antimatter containment and nuclear reactor cooling rods.
- **The Local Politics:** A fractured world. A massive, heavily armed Rebel Group operates in the frozen peaks, actively fighting for the Orion Empire against the planet's native defenders.

### 6. Tarnaxis (The Scorch Canyons)
- **The Concept:** A highly advanced planet scarred by continent-spanning canyons. The surface is entirely uninhabitable due to lethal solar radiation and heat.
- **Environment & Hazards:** Players stepping onto the surface without specialized Void Thruster shielding will incinerate. All gameplay, cities, and wars occur in the massive, sprawling underground networks built into the canyon walls.
- **Key Resources:** Geothermal energy cores and deep-crust alien minerals.
- **The Local Politics:** Divided. The underground societies are split, with powerful Evil-aligned clans openly supporting the Orion Empire's war effort.
- **The Mission:** A logistical and diplomatic crawl. Players travel from canyon to canyon, trading critical resources with Neutral and Good-aligned underground societies. The journey is broken up by intense, close-quarters skirmishes against the hostile Tarnaxis clans guarding the canyon bridges and tunnels.
- **The Core Encounter:** The final objective is a massive subterranean assault. The player and their NPC squad must descend into the deepest levels of the crust to physically destroy a heavily fortified **Orion Colony** that serves as a forward operating base for the Empire.

### 7. Qirellon (The Hive Woods)
- **The Concept:** A planet entirely covered in impossibly tall, dense alien pine forests. The atmosphere is perpetually dim under the canopy.
- **Environment & Hazards:** Navigating the dense, 3D labyrinth of the forest canopy requires Jetpacks or Elytras. Falling to the forest floor means facing the apex predators that lurk in the dark.
- **The Inhabitants (The Hivemind):** The native aliens are tall, slender, and operate as a unified, highly intelligent super-society. They act as a single consciousness.
- **The Local Politics:** The Qirellon Hivemind is fully allied with the Orion Empire, providing them with biological technology and synchronized tactical superiority.
- **The Mission (The Hacking Tutorial):** The planetary mission is heavily focused on aerial and cyber warfare. The player starts with several high-altitude air scout missions over the canopy. The primary objective is to execute a massive spaceship air raid on the Hivemind's capital.
- **The Core Encounter:** Before the air raid can succeed, the player must physically infiltrate and hack the Qirellon Iron Dome defense network. This sequence serves as the server's official, high-stakes tutorial on how to use Decryption Arrays and the 64-channel hacking minigames. If the player succeeds, the Iron Dome falls, the air raid connects, and the Hivemind is broken.
- **Post-Story Exploration:** After the war, exploring the deep, dark patches of Qirellon's forest floor yields some of the most amazing loot and rare biological resources on the server — provided players can survive the predators lurking below the canopy.

### 8. Nytharix (The Neon Bastion)
- **The Concept:** A highly advanced, sprawling Cyberpunk metropolis planet. The pinnacle of "Good" aligned civilization in the Zeratari system.
- **Environment & Hazards:** Neon-lit skyscrapers, flying cars, and massive data-hubs. Urban warfare environments.
- **The Local Politics (The Republic):** A Democratic Republic currently fighting a desperate, losing war for survival. Nytharix and Myrikane are the last bastions standing against the Orion Empire, the Qirellon Hivemind, the Tarnaxis evil clans, and the Kyrothis rebels.
- **The Mission (The Gear Up):** The player and their NPC squad arrive in the Republic to consolidate forces. The mission begins as a massive logistical and diplomatic effort — trading resources, unlocking top-tier cybernetic upgrades, and gearing up for what is assumed to be an inevitable escalation with Orion.
- **The Core Encounter (The Blackout & The Escape):** The escalation happens instantly and catastrophically. The Orion Empire launches a massive **Rogue AI** attack that successfully DDoS's the entire Nytharix planetary grid. 
  - *The Blackout:* All technology fails. Void Terminals, 64-channel redstone networks, and Teleporters are completely disabled by the plugin.
  - *The Escape:* Stranded on a suddenly dark, helpless planet under imminent Orion invasion, the player must navigate the chaotic, powerless cyberpunk streets. They are tasked with locating an old-world, functional solar-powered escape pod to launch themselves back to Tarnaxis for safety.
- **The Escalation Point:** The moment the player successfully launches the escape pod and survives the Nytharix blackout, the conflict's status instantly upgrades. The server automatically reclassifies the war as a **Galactic War**, broadcasting the subtitle to all 34 dimensions that the Solar System has formally entered the Andromeda conflict in defense of the Republic.

### 9. Orion (The Final World - Good) / (The First World - Evil)
- **The Concept:** The pinnacle of the Zeratari System. Orion is a hyper-advanced, Authoritarian Cyberpunk Empire. The planet itself has been heavily industrialized and weaponized to the point that it resembles a planetary Death Star.
- **The Orion Empire:** They are waging total war to conquer all of Zeratari. They are ruthless, technologically superior, and desperate for resources to fuel their war machine.
- **The Unholy Alliance:** The Emperor of Orion has forged a dark pact with **El Diablo**, believing the Lord of the Abyss will help them locate the "Solar System" to strip-mine Earth and its neighboring planets for fresh resources.
- **The Good Core Encounter:** Players must breach the planetary defenses, navigate the mechanized core of Orion, and face the Emperor and the true master behind the corruption in the Ultimate Showdown.

## 10. The Evil Campaign (The Dark Mirror)

For players who accepted El Diablo's Wither Rose in Chapter 2, their journey through the Andromeda galaxy is entirely inverted. They do not fight to save the universe; they fight to conquer it.

### The Orion Arrival
While Good players arrive on Myrikane to build a desperate resistance, Evil players use their Demon Cores to arrive directly on **Orion**. They are welcomed not as heroes, but as useful mercenaries and dark emissaries of El Diablo. Their mission control is not Professor Krystal, but high-ranking **Orion Imperial Officers** and El Diablo himself.

### The Andromeda Conquest (Inverted)
The Evil progression path mirrors the Good path perfectly, separated only by the veil of morality:

1. **Velkorn (The Scorched Citadel):**
   - *Evil Mission:* Arrive on Velkorn as Orion diplomats/enforcers. The player must speak to the various brute clans, bribe them, intimidate them, or physically crush the dissenting factions to force the Velkorans to join El Diablo's side in the coming war.

2. **Zeruun (The Acidic Empire):**
   - *Evil Mission:* Secure the chemical weapons *for* Orion. The PvPvE convergence happens here, where Evil players must defend the vats or secure the payload against the incoming Good infiltration squad.

3. **Kyrothis (The Frozen Zenith):**
   - *Evil Mission:* Arm and support the Rebel faction. Participate in the massive aerial dogfight on the Evil side to secure the Blue Ice highways for the Orion war machine.

4. **Tarnaxis (The Scorch Canyons):**
   - *Evil Mission:* Defend the Orion Colony from subterranean assaults. Hunt down the Neutral/Good canyon societies and force them into submission or wipe them out to secure the geothermal cores.

5. **Qirellon (The Hive Woods):**
   - *Evil Mission:* Protect the Iron Dome. Counter-hack the incoming Republic forces. The player must ensure the Hivemind remains fully synchronized with the Orion military grid.

6. **Nytharix (The Neon Bastion):**
   - *Evil Mission:* **Execute the Blackout.** The player is the one who physically deploys the Rogue AI virus into the Nytharix central mainframe. They must fight through the Republic's elite guards, plant the virus, and watch the city go dark. This act officially triggers the **Galactic War**.

7. **Myrikane (The Dark Mirror):**
   - *Evil Mission:* The final push. While Good players are making their desperate final assault on Orion, Evil players are launching a full-scale planetary invasion of Myrikane to crush the Republic's forward operating base and eliminate Professor Krystal's command structure.
   - *The Catch (The Inevitable Fall):* Regardless of how hard the Evil players fight on Myrikane, they receive an emergency broadcast from El Diablo: **Orion is falling.** The Good players have breached the core. To complete the Evil campaign, the Evil players must immediately recall to Orion for a desperate last stand.
   - *The Final Stand:* In the crumbling, exploding core of Orion, Evil players face the Good assault force. To "pass" this mission, Evil players must kill all of the Good NPC Allies and reduce the human Good party down to **exactly 1 surviving player**. However, for lore reasons, Orion always falls. Even if Evil completes their objective, the core explodes, the planet is destroyed, and they are pulled into the final event.

### The Reality Checkerboard (The Final Confrontation)
Regardless of whether they successfully burned Myrikane or failed to defend Orion, the climax remains the same. The Lord intervenes. The Evil players are pulled onto the Black side of the Reality Checkerboard alongside the Orion Grunts. They fight the 8 Good players, Professor Kraft, and Professor Krystal. 

And in the end, they watch The Lord banish their master from the code itself, earning the title of **Demon**.
