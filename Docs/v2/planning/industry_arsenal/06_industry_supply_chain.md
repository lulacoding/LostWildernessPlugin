# Lost Wilderness: Industry & Supply Chain Deep Dive

## 1. The Oil Refinery Output Tree
Oil is not just refined into Diesel. The refinery produces a full tree of byproducts:

- **Diesel** — Primary vehicle fuel
- **Aviation Fuel** — Planes, helicopters, rockets
- **Rubber** — Combined with Sulfur to produce vulcanised rubber for tyres
- **Plastic/Polymer** — Gun stocks, vehicle body panels, circuit board housings
- **Pitch** — Black tar byproduct used in road construction and waterproofing
- **Cordite** — Stabilised explosive propellant for guns and missiles
- **Lubricant** — Reduces factory machine wear; extends vehicle engine life

## 2. The Road System (Pitch + Asphalt)
- **Pitch Production:** Extracted as a byproduct of the Oil Refinery process. Cannot be obtained any other way.
- **Asphalt Blocks:** Pitch is combined with gravel and sand in a Mixing Vat machine to produce custom Asphalt blocks (dark, smooth road surface).
- **Road Placement:** Players lay Asphalt blocks as a physical road surface between their settlements, bases, and factories.
- **Vehicle Bonuses on Roads:**
  - *Tyre Durability:* Tyres degrade significantly slower on Asphalt vs. raw terrain.
  - *Speed Bonus:* Vehicles (Jeeps, Tanks, Cargo Trucks) move faster on Asphalt roads.
  - *Fuel Efficiency:* Smooth road surface reduces engine load, extending fuel range by a percentage.
- **The Meta:** Clans that invest in road networks between their factories and cities gain a massive long-term logistical advantage — faster supply chains, cheaper tyre replacement, and lower fuel costs. Road infrastructure becomes a visible sign of a civilization's advancement.
- **Road Destruction:** Roads can be physically destroyed by explosions or mining. Cutting an enemy's road network during a siege is a valid tactical move — slowing their tank reinforcements and starving their supply lines.

## 3. Expanded Books (Epochian Tomes)
- **The Problem:** Vanilla Book and Quill is limited to 50 pages. The Lost Wilderness Bible, clan constitutions, and war histories require far more.
- **The Solution:** `PluginV2` introduces **Epochian Tomes** — custom NBT-backed book items capable of holding thousands of pages.
- **Use Cases:**
  - The full written server Bible (to be supplied by the developer)
  - Grand Constitutions of Solar Empires and Galactic Empires
  - War histories and post-match chronicles
  - Player-written lore books, religious texts, and propaganda pamphlets
  - Technical manuals for operating 64-channel redstone computers
