**PluginV2 – Personality Trait & Elemental Quest System**

─────── PART 1 ───────

**Overview**
- Take a **10-question personality quiz** in the lobby on first join to discover your **Personality Trait** and **Elemental Affinity**.
- Progress through **4 tiers** (Apprentice → Trait → Master → Ultimate) as you complete story milestones from 0% to 300%.
- Unlock **passive abilities** at each tier that enhance your playstyle.
- Receive a **Trait Ultimate Item** at 100% completion (legendary weapon/armor with custom enchants).
- Discover **5 Elemental Temples** and unlock **god-tier elemental passives** at 300% completion.
- Bless Ultimate items with **13 Holy Enchants** for additional power.

**The 13 Personality Traits**

**Combat-Focused Traits:**
1. **MAGE** – Master of arcane magic and potions
   - **Apprentice (0%):** Splash potions have +50% radius
   - **Trait (25%):** Potions stack duration instead of replacing
   - **Master (50%):** Potion effects last +50% longer
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Mage's Focus Bow** (Power X, Infinity, Flame III, homing arrows)

2. **WARRIOR** – Melee combat specialist
   - **Apprentice (0%):** +10% melee damage with swords
   - **Trait (25%):** Shield cooldown -50%
   - **Master (50%):** +20% damage when using shield
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Warlord's Blade** (Sharpness X, Knockback V, Fire Aspect III)

3. **ARCHER** – Ranged combat expert
   - **Apprentice (0%):** +15% projectile damage
   - **Trait (25%):** Arrows pierce +1 entity
   - **Master (50%):** +25% arrow speed
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Celestial Bow** (Power X, Infinity, Punch V, homing arrows)

4. **BERSERKER** – Brutal close-combat fighter
   - **Apprentice (0%):** Gain Strength I when <30% HP
   - **Trait (25%):** +20% damage when not wearing chestplate
   - **Master (50%):** -25% damage taken at low HP
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Ragnarok Axe** (Sharpness X, Efficiency X, Sweeping Edge V)

─────── PART 2 ───────

**Support & Utility Traits:**
5. **HEALER** – Master of restoration (Alignment-based: Good = Regen aura, Evil = Wither aura)
   - **Apprentice (0%):** AoE healing/damage every 15s (alignment-based)
   - **Trait (25%):** Potion effects last +50% longer
   - **Master (50%):** +100% potion healing
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Flask of Eternity** (Grants random positive potion effect)

6. **RANGER** – Nature and animal companion specialist
   - **Apprentice (0%):** +20% movement speed in Forest/Jungle biomes
   - **Trait (25%):** Tamed mobs deal +15% damage
   - **Master (50%):** +30% taming success rate
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Ranger's Quiver** (Protection X, Thorns V, Unbreaking X)

7. **ALCHEMIST** – Potion brewing expert
   - **Apprentice (0%):** Brewed potions last +25% longer
   - **Trait (25%):** Can brew 4 potions simultaneously
   - **Master (50%):** Brewing stand never requires fuel
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Philosopher's Stone** (Grants brewing bonuses)

8. **SMITH** – Master craftsman and repairer
   - **Apprentice (0%):** Anvil costs -2 levels
   - **Trait (25%):** -20% material cost for gear crafting
   - **Master (50%):** Tools lose durability 50% slower
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Eternal Hammer** (Efficiency X, Unbreaking X, 50% repair chance)

9. **SCOUT** – Speed and stealth specialist
   - **Apprentice (0%):** Permanent Speed I (+15%)
   - **Trait (25%):** Sneak movement = walk speed
   - **Master (50%):** -75% fall damage
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Shadowstep Boots** (Feather Falling X, Depth Strider X, double-sneak teleport)

─────── PART 3 ───────

**Specialized Traits:**
10. **SAGE** – Knowledge and experience master
   - **Apprentice (0%):** +25% XP from all sources
   - **Trait (25%):** +1 extra enchant option at enchanting table
   - **Master (50%):** -50% enchanting cost
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Tome of Wisdom** (Grants 5 XP levels on use)

11. **TAMER** – Animal companion specialist
   - **Apprentice (0%):** +25% taming success rate
   - **Trait (25%):** Tamed mobs have +30% max HP
   - **Master (50%):** Tamed mobs regenerate health faster
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Ancient Whistle** (Summons wolf companion)

12. **RUNEKEEPER** – Enchantment specialist
   - **Apprentice (0%):** Enchanted items glow with particles, +10% enchant effectiveness
   - **Trait (25%):** No lapis required for enchanting
   - **Master (50%):** +2 enchant levels on all gear
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Runeblade** (Sharpness X, all enchants, random crit effects)

13. **ILLUSIONIST** – Deception and misdirection expert
   - **Apprentice (0%):** `/trait decoy` spawns fake player (5s, 2min cooldown)
   - **Trait (25%):** Invisibility potions last 3x longer
   - **Master (50%):** Mobs ignore you when invisible
   - **Ultimate (100%):** Receive **Mirror Shard** (Swap positions with target player)

**Progression Tiers**
- **Apprentice (0%):** Starting tier, basic passive ability
- **Trait (25%):** Unlock at 25% story completion, improved passive
- **Master (50%):** Unlock at 50% story completion, advanced passive
- **Ultimate (100%):** Unlock at 100% story completion, receive Trait Ultimate Item + max passive

─────── PART 4 ───────

**The 5 Elemental Affinities**

Your element is secretly assigned during the personality quiz but remains hidden until a Lord reveals it to you. Once revealed, you can discover 5 Elemental Temples to unlock god-tier passives.

**FIRE** – Passion and destruction
- **Passive (300%):** Immune to fire and lava damage
- **Temple:** Hidden in Nether fortress

**EARTH** – Stability and endurance
- **Passive (300%):** Immune to suffocation damage
- **Temple:** Hidden deep underground

**WIND** – Freedom and mobility
- **Passive (300%):** Elytra never loses durability
- **Temple:** Hidden at high altitude

**WATER** – Flow and adaptability
- **Passive (300%):** Permanent Water Breathing, immune to drowning
- **Temple:** Hidden in ocean monument

**AETHER** – Balance and transcendence
- **Passive (300%):** -90% fall damage, levitation effects last longer
- **Temple:** Hidden in End dimension

**Post-Game State (300% Completion)**
- Complete ALL 5 Elemental Temples
- Reach 300% story completion (100% story + 100% advancements + 100% temples)
- All 5 elemental passives become active simultaneously
- Receive +50% bonus drops/XP from all world events
- Title: "⭐ Post-Game God" displayed in `/datejoined`

─────── PART 5 ───────

**Holy Enchants System**

Ultimate items can be **blessed** by Lords to receive **Holy Enchants** (custom enchantments with unique effects):

1. **SOULFIRE** – Attacks set targets on fire + bonus fire damage
2. **DIVINE_SHIELD** – Blocking grants absorption hearts
3. **CELESTIAL_STRIKE** – 20% chance to summon lightning on hit
4. **VOID_PIERCE** – Ignores 50% of target's armor
5. **NATURES_GRASP** – Hits root targets in place for 3 seconds
6. **THUNDERCLAP** – Sprint attacks create knockback wave
7. **SERPENTS_FANG** – Attacks apply stacking poison
8. **LUNAR_BLESSING** – Heal 1 heart when killing under moonlight
9. **STARFALL** – Charged attacks rain down star projectiles
10. **ANCIENT_WARD** – Reduce all damage by 2 flat hearts
11. **PHOENIX_FLAME** – 10% chance to revive with 3 hearts (once per day)
12. **TITANIC_FORCE** – Attacks deal +10% of target's max HP as damage
13. **ECHO_STEP** – Leave teleport trail that lasts 5 seconds

**Blessing Mechanic:**
- Lord uses `/trait bless <player>` while player holds Ultimate item
- Enchantment levels doubled (up to hard cap)
- Random Holy Enchant added to item
- Can only bless each item once

**Christmas Event (Dec 25):**
- Players at 300% completion receive **9 random Holy Enchants** as gifts
- Can be applied to any Ultimate items
- Once per year per player

─────── PART 6 ───────

**Completion % Calculation**

**0-100%: Story Milestones**
- Nether Portal: 10%
- Ender Dragon: 20%
- 36 Wither Kills: 30%
- 6 Devoider Kills: 40%
- El Diablo Kill: 50%
- Heavenly Tower: 70%
- Lord's Plateau: 100%

**100-200%: Vanilla Advancements**
- Each advancement completed adds to completion %
- Includes all Minecraft advancements (Story, Nether, End, Adventure, Husbandry)

**200-300%: Elemental Temples**
- Each of 5 temples completed: +20%
- Fire Temple: 220%
- Earth Temple: 240%
- Wind Temple: 260%
- Water Temple: 280%
- Aether Temple: 300%

**Commands**
- `/trait info [player]` – View trait, tier, element status, completion %
- `/trait revealelement <player>` – Lord only: Reveal element to player
- `/trait bless <player>` – Lord only: Bless held Ultimate item with Holy Enchant
- `/trait temple <element>` – Admin: Mark temple complete
- `/trait set <trait>` – Admin: Force trait assignment
- `/datejoined` – Extended to show trait/element info after zodiac

**Permissions**
- **lw.rank.lord** – Reveal elements and bless items
- **op** – Admin commands (temple, set, quiz reset)

─────── PART 7 ───────

**How to Progress**

1. **First Join:** Take personality quiz in lobby (10 questions, movement frozen)
2. **0-25%:** Play as Apprentice, basic passive unlocked
3. **25%:** Reach 25% completion → auto-advance to **Trait** tier
4. **50%:** Reach 50% completion → auto-advance to **Master** tier
5. **100%:** Reach 100% completion → auto-advance to **Ultimate** tier, receive Trait Ultimate Item
6. **Element Reveal:** Ask a Lord to run `/trait revealelement` to discover your element
7. **Temple Quests:** Complete 5 Elemental Temple BetonQuest chains
8. **300%:** Complete all temples + all advancements → **Post-Game God** status

**Database Tables**
- `player_traits` – Trait assignment, tier, element reveal status
- `player_temples` – Temple completion tracking (5 rows per player)
- `player_quiz_answers` – Quiz analytics (10 rows per player)
- `player_holy_enchants` – Holy Enchant grants tracking
- `player_christmas_claims` – Christmas event claim tracking

**Technical Details**
- **Module:** `personality` in `config/core.yml` enabled-modules
- **PDC Storage:** Trait enchants stored in `lw:trait_enchant` key
- **Holy Enchants:** Stored in `lw:holy_enchant_1`, `lw:holy_enchant_2`, etc.
- **Lore Display:** Purple text for Trait enchants, gold text for Holy enchants
- **Event Integration:** `EventServiceImpl` checks post-game status for reward multiplier
- **Quiz Integration:** Lobby plugin integration (quiz files not yet created)
