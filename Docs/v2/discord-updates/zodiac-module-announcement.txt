**🌟 NEW MODULE: Zodiac Star Sign System 🌌**

The Zodiac module is now fully implemented in RPG Core V2! This comprehensive astrological system adds deep personalization and strategic gameplay bonuses.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
**📊 OVERVIEW**

Every player is assigned zodiac signs based on their **MC calendar join date**:
• **Month Sign** - Based on the day/month you joined
• **Year Sign** - Based on the year you joined
• **13 Total Signs** - Aries ♈ through Ophiuchus ⛎

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**🦊 SPIRIT ANIMALS (14 Total)**

Each player has a **hidden spirit animal** that provides passive bonuses:

• **Wolf** - +5% melee damage near wolves/packs
• **Fox** - +10% speed at night
• **Bat** - Regeneration I in darkness (light ≤4)
• **Dolphin** - Enhanced Dolphin's Grace near water
• **Bee** - Crops grow faster in 5-block radius
• **Cat** - Phantoms never target you
• **Rabbit** - Jump Boost I when health <30%
• **Parrot** - Hostile mob detection (louder footsteps)
• **Axolotl** - Regeneration after combat
• **Panda** - Slowness immunity
• **Turtle** - Resistance I when stationary 3+ seconds
• **Ocelot** - Creepers remain neutral to you
• **Horse** - Speed boost in Plains/Savanna biomes
• **Iron Golem** - Increased knockback resistance

**Bonus:** All spirit animals grant **+20% drop chance** from their corresponding mob type!

Some signs have **different perks** based on your honor:

**How it works:**
• Alignment = Your **Honor Score** from Reputation system
• Positive honor (≥0) = Good

**Signs with variants:**
• Libra ♎ (trade bonuses vs cheaper prices)
• Scorpio ♏ (AoE healing vs stealth)
• Ophiuchus ⛎ (healing aura vs serpent form)

**💬 COMMANDS**

**`/datejoined`** (extended)
Shows your join date + zodiac info:
• Month Sign + symbol
• Year Sign + symbol
• Epochian status (if applicable)
• 2nd Sign (hidden or revealed)
• Spirit Animal (hidden or revealed)

**`/zodiac info [player]`**
View detailed zodiac information:
• Month & year signs
• Active bonuses (month active, year active, sync!)
• Epochian status
• Revealed spirit animal
• Current celestial alignments

**`/zodiac reveal <player>`** (Lord only)
Reveal hidden secrets:
• Epochian 2nd sign
• Spirit animal
• Sends mystical messages to both players
• **Permission:** `lw.rank.lord`

**`/zodiac clanbonus`**
Check clan-wide year bonus:
• Shows your clan leader
• Indicates if year bonus is active
• Lists benefits (Luck I + 5% XP)

**🎮 HOW IT WORKS**

**On First Join:**
1. Your join date is recorded in the MC calendar
2. Month sign calculated from day/month
3. Year sign calculated from year
4. Random spirit animal assigned (hidden)
5. If Day 0: Epochian status + hidden 2nd sign

**Passive Effects:**
• Zodiac perks apply automatically every second
• Spirit animal effects are always active (even when hidden!)
• No need to equip or activate anything
• Works alongside all other buffs

**Lord Reveals:**
• Only Lord-rank players can reveal hidden info
• Use `/zodiac reveal <player>` to unlock:
  - Epochian 2nd sign
  - Spirit animal identity
• Once revealed, stays revealed forever!

**📊 TECHNICAL DETAILS**

**Database:** `player_zodiac` table
• Stores: month_sign, year_sign, is_epochian, second_sign, spirit_animal, revealed states
• MySQL + H2 compatible
• Async operations for performance

**Integration:**
• Calendar system (MC date/time)
• Reputation system (Good/Evil alignment)
• Clan system (leader year bonus)
• Player profiles (join date tracking)

**Performance:**
• Profiles cached in memory (instant access)
• Effects applied every 1 second (not every tick)
• Potion effects refresh with 5-second duration
• Zero lag impact!

**🌟 LORE & FLAVOR**

The zodiac system ties into the Lost Wilderness mythology:

**Epochians** - Players who witnessed the first sunrise on Day 0 of the new calendar. They are blessed by the cosmos with dual signs, marking them as chosen vessels of celestial power.

**Ophiuchus** - The 13th constellation, hidden from most calendars, represents the serpent bearer and healer. Only those born in the Ophiuchus window or on the sacred Epoch Day 0 can channel its power.

**Spirit Animals** - Each soul is bound to a guardian spirit from the wilderness. These animals guide their bonded companions, granting them supernatural abilities even when their true form remains hidden.

**Sync Bonus** - When the celestial spheres align and a player's birth signs match the current cosmic configuration, they achieve perfect harmony with the universe, doubling their astrological power.
