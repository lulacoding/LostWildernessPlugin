─────── PART 1 ───────

**⚖️ Reputation System – Red Dead Redemption Style Honor**
We have introduced a massive new lore-driven **Honor System**. Your actions in the world now carry weight, tracking whether you are a protector of the innocent or a force of darkness.

**🌟 Honor Score & Titles**
- Your overall **Honor Score** scales from **-1000** (Pure Evil) to **+1000** (Pure Good).
- Depending on your score, you will receive a dynamic **Title**: `Hero`, `Noble`, `Honorable`, `Neutral`, `Dishonorable`, `Villain`, or `Outlaw`.
- Use the **`/reputation`** command to view a beautiful visual progress bar of your Honor, your current Title, and your exact standing with the five major factions.

**🏰 Factions & Alignments**

Your actions now shift your standing with these major factions:

- **Celestial** – Pure light and divine order. Protectors of the innocent and enemy of all corruption.
- **The Redeemed** – Mortals who answer the gods’ call and walk the path of redemption. Even villains can fight their way back into this faction’s favor.
- **Wildlands** – Neutral spirits of nature, beasts and druids. They judge you by how you treat the world itself.
- **Corrupted** – Void‑touched cults and those who pledge themselves to the Devoider and other dark powers.
- **Destroyers** – Raiders, griefers and warbands who live for chaos, bloodshed and destruction.

> **Epochians** are *players*, not an NPC faction – those who were here since the very start of the world (Day 0).
> Their Honor can still swing between Hero and Outlaw; being an Epochian just marks you as an original.

─────── PART 2 ───────

**🗡️ Good Deeds & Heroism**
- **Boss Kills**: Defeating world bosses like the *Devoider* or *El Diablo* grants massive reputation with the Celestial and Epochian factions.
- **Saving Souls**: Curing a Zombie Villager provides a huge surge of Honor (+100).
- **Justice & Prosperity**: Killing hostile monsters grants small bonuses to Wildlands reputation. Trading with villagers slightly boosts your Celestial standing.

**💀 Bad Deeds & Villainy**
- **Murder**: Killing other players in PvP instantly destroys your Celestial honor and grants you standing with the Destroyers.
- **Griefing Innocents**: Killing peaceful creatures (Villagers, Horses, Pets, Iron Golems) penalizes your honor significantly.

**🌑 Pledging to the Darkness**
- Players can actively choose to side with the bosses instead of fighting them.
- If you approach a Boss (Wither) and right-click them with a **Wither Rose**, you will **pledge your soul to the darkness**. This instantly tanks your Celestial reputation (-150) and grants you massive Corrupted standing (+100).

**Live Feedback**
- Whenever your standing improves or worsens with a faction, you will receive subtle gray chat notifications letting you know your actions were noticed.
