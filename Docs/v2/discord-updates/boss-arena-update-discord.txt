─────── PART 1 ───────

**Overview**
- Boss system transforms **Withers** spawned on the **Nether roof** (Y=127/128) into powerful custom bosses if kill requirements are met.
- **Two bosses:** **Devoider** (Survival world) and **El Diablo** (Amplified world).
- **Arena system** creates sandboxed boss fight areas with barrier walls and boundary enforcement.
- **Kill tracking** stores player and clan kills in database for progression milestones.
- **Custom drops** include Nether Stars, XP multipliers, and reputation rewards.

**The Bosses**

**Devoider** (Survival)
- **Requirements:** Survive for **36 Wither kills** (tracked per player)
- **Transformation:** When you spawn a Wither on Nether roof (Y=127/128) after 36 kills, it transforms into **Devoider**
- **Health:** 600 HP (2x normal Wither)
- **Abilities:** Standard Wither attacks + enhanced projectiles
- **Drops:** 3 Nether Stars, bonus XP, high reputation gain
- **Milestone:** Unlocks `MILESTONE_WITHER_36` progression reward

**El Diablo** (Amplified)
- **Requirements:** Kill **6 Devoiders** (tracked per player)
- **Transformation:** Spawn Wither on Nether roof after 6 Devoider kills → transforms into **El Diablo**
- **Health:** 900 HP (3x normal Wither)
- **Abilities:**
  - Standard Wither attacks
  - **Horde Summon:** At 50% HP, spawns 10 zombies, 5 skeletons, 3 wither skeletons in arena
  - Enhanced projectile damage
- **Drops:** 5 Nether Stars, massive XP, highest reputation gain
- **Arena:** Automatically creates bedrock platform arena with barrier walls
- **Milestone:** Unlocks `MILESTONE_EL_DIABLO` progression reward

─────── PART 2 ───────

**Arena System**
- **Creation:** Arenas are created programmatically when bosses spawn (or via `/arena-test create` for testing)
- **Boundary:** AABB (axis-aligned bounding box) containment system prevents players from leaving during fight
- **Barriers:** Temporary barrier walls placed around arena perimeter, removed after boss dies
- **Sandbox Mode:** Restricted arenas prevent escape until boss is defeated
- **Visual Feedback:** Impact particles when players try to leave boundary

**Kill Tracking**
- **Database:** `boss_kills` table tracks individual player kills and clan kills for each boss type
- **Types:** WITHER (normal), DEVOIDER, EL_DIABLO
- **Clan Credit:** If killer is in a clan, clan also receives kill credit
- **Progression Integration:** Killing bosses unlocks milestones via `ProgressionModule`

**Diablo Lair Generation**
- **Command:** `/diablo-lair` (requires permission `lw.boss.diablo-lair`)
- **Function:** Generates a **31x31 bedrock platform** 50 blocks below your current position
- **Purpose:** Creates flat arena for El Diablo fight in Nether
- **Usage:** Run in Nether at desired location before spawning boss

**Custom Drops**
- **Wither:** Normal drops (Nether Star + some coal)
- **Devoider:** 3 Nether Stars + 500 bonus XP + high reputation
- **El Diablo:** 5 Nether Stars + 1000 bonus XP + highest reputation
- **War Mode:** If killer and victim are in warring clans, victim's head also drops

─────── PART 3 ───────

**Commands**
- `/diablo-lair` – Generate bedrock platform 50 blocks below you (permission: `lw.boss.diablo-lair`)
- `/arena-test create` – Create test arena at your location (admin only, permission: `lw.boss.arena-test`)
- `/arena-test show` – Show arena boundaries (admin only)
- `/arena-test clear` – Remove all test arenas (admin only)

**Permissions**
- **lw.boss.diablo-lair** – Generate Diablo lair platforms
- **lw.boss.arena-test** – Admin arena testing commands

**How to Progress**
1. **Step 1:** Kill 36 normal Withers anywhere in the world
2. **Step 2:** Go to Nether roof (Y=127/128) and spawn a Wither → transforms into **Devoider**
3. **Step 3:** Kill Devoider 6 times (track via `/progress` or `/milestones`)
4. **Step 4:** Run `/diablo-lair` in Nether to generate arena platform
5. **Step 5:** Spawn Wither on Nether roof → transforms into **El Diablo**
6. **Step 6:** Defeat El Diablo in arena for ultimate rewards

**Tips**
- **Team up:** Boss fights are challenging, bring clan members
- **Prepare:** Stock potions, enchanted gear, and golden apples
- **Arena:** El Diablo fight is contained, you can't escape once it starts
- **Horde:** At 50% HP, El Diablo summons adds – prepare AOE weapons
- **Tracking:** Check your kill counts with `/class info` or database queries

**Technical Details**
- **Module:** `boss` in `config/core.yml` enabled-modules
- **Boss Transform:** `RoofWitherListener` checks spawn location (Y=127/128) and kill requirements
- **Arena Manager:** In-memory registry of active arenas with boundary enforcement
- **Database:** `boss_kills` table (player_uuid, boss_type, kill_count, last_kill_time, clan_uuid)
- **Integration:** ProgressionModule, ReputationModule, ClansModule for rewards and tracking
