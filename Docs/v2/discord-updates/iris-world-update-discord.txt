**⚙️ Technical — Biome Painting Compatibility**

The seasonal biome painting system has been updated to protect volcanic terrain.

**Problem identified:**
The biome painting system transforms chunks seasonally (e.g. Spring → Flower Forest, Winter → Snowy Plains). Without protection, volcanic biomes would be overwritten — a caldera would turn into a flower forest in spring.

**Root cause:**
Iris volcanic biomes use two vanilla derivatives:
- `THE_VOID` — used by tropical/volcanoes, volcanoes-lava, volcanic-plains
- `DESERT` — used by all terralost volcanic biomes (crater, caldera, peaks, ashen-savanna, basalt-cliffs)

BiomePainter reads vanilla derivatives and would overwrite both.

**Fix applied:**
Added `DESERT` and `THE_VOID` to the `excluded-biomes` list in `RPG_Core_V2/config/core.yml`. These biomes will never be repainted regardless of season.

**Event biome interaction (known behaviour):**
- `SummerHeatwaveEvent` fires in DESERT biomes → volcanic-crater/caldera/basalt-cliffs will trigger it in summer ✅ intentional — volcanic areas ARE hot
- `FrostEvent` fires in SNOWY biomes → will NOT fire in volcanic areas ✅ correct
- THE_VOID biomes are not in any event group → pure volcanoes trigger no events ✅ correct

**Status:** Biome painting exclusions live in config. Painting is currently disabled (painting-enabled: false) and will be enabled in a future session when seasonal testing begins.

**📅 UPDATE – 2026-03-25**

**🗺️ World Biome Guide — Complete Biome List**

Here's every biome you can explore across the Lost Wilderness survival world. The world is divided into **10 regions**, each with its own terrain style, climate, and feel.

---

**🌋 Spawn Zone — Volcanic Region** *(~800 blocks from 0,0)*
This is where you start. A dramatic volcanic landscape surrounding the Spawn Village.

🌿 Volcanic Plains — flat open grassland at 0,0, home to the Spawn Village
🌋 Volcanoes — towering black mountains with basalt peaks and drifting smoke
🔥 Lava Craters — volcanoes filled with lava lakes in their cores
🏜️ Ash Plains — wide flat stretches of grey ash between the craters
🕳️ Volcanic Crater — collapsed calderas with sheer inner walls
⛰️ Volcanic Peaks — jagged spires of volcanic rock
🌑 Caldera — enormous bowl-shaped collapsed volcanoes
🖤 Basalt Cliffs — sheer walls of black basalt rising from the plains

---

**🌲 Forests Region**
Dense multi-biome forest zone with varied tree types and terrain.

🌲 Autumn Forest — orange/red leaf canopy, tundra feel
🌿 Redwood Forest — towering mega-trees with thick canopy
⛰️ Mountain Plains — open highland plains between forested peaks
🌸 Violet Reaching Forest — tall trees with purple-tinted undergrowth
🌻 Flower Forest — carpet of flowers across rolling hills
🌳 Birch Highlands — tall birch trees on elevated terrain
🍁 Osaka Red Forest — red-leaved Japanese maple aesthetic
🏔️ Mountain Peaks — high rocky summits above the treeline
🌳 Mixed Forest — combined oak/birch/spruce woodland

---

**❄️ Frozen Region**
Ice, snow, and frozen landscapes. Harsh but beautiful.

🌨️ Frozen Plains — flat snowfields
❄️ Ice Spikes — towering columns of packed ice
🌲 Frozen Pine Forest — spruce forest under heavy snow
🌲 Pine Hills — rolling snowy hills covered in pines
🏔️ Frozen Mountains — massive snow-capped peaks
🏔️ Ice Spike Mountains — extreme mountain terrain with ice formations
🌬️ Snowy Spruce Hills — gentler spruce hills with snowfall
🌊 Frozen Ocean — ice-covered ocean with icebergs
🏖️ Ice Beach — snowy shoreline

---

**🌵 Hot / Desert Region**
Arid badlands, desert dunes, and scorched terrain.

🏜️ Desert Dunes — rolling sand dunes with sparse vegetation
🔴 Red Dunes — deep red sand desert
🌵 Desert Oasis — rare water source amid the dunes
⛰️ Desert Mountains — towering cliff formations rising from sand
🌊 Desert Ocean — warm turquoise waters off the desert coast
🏖️ Desert Beach — sandy shore between desert and sea

---

**🍄 Mushroom Region**
Rare, eerie fungal biomes. Hostile mobs don't spawn here.

🍄 Mushroom Forest — giant red and brown mushrooms covering the land
🌿 Mushroom Plains — open flat area with mushroom clusters
🟣 Warped Forest — blue-green nether-inspired fungal woodland
🔴 Crimson Forest — red fungal forest with warm lighting
🌊 Mushroom Lake — calm inland water surrounded by fungi

---

**🌿 Temperate Region**
The most varied zone — classic woodland and plains with many sub-biomes.

🌳 Oak Forest — standard oak woodland
🌿 Lush Plains — vibrant green open fields
🌸 Cherry Blossom Forest — pink canopy sakura trees
🌺 Sakura Forest — denser sakura with flower undergrowth
🌸 Osaka Violet Forest — violet-themed Japanese maple forest
🌼 Flower Fields — open plains blanketed in wildflowers
🌳 Birch Forest — white-barked birch woodland
🌿 Highlands — elevated open plateau terrain
🏔️ Temperate Plateau — flat-topped highland with cliff edges
🌊 Temperate Ocean — standard deep blue open water

---

**🌴 Tropical Region**
Lush rainforests, coral reefs, and jungle mountains.

🌿 Rainforest — dense jungle canopy with heavy undergrowth
🏔️ Jungle Mountains — steep rainforest peaks
🎋 Bamboo Forest — dense bamboo groves
🌊 Coral Ocean — shallow reef waters with vibrant coral
🏖️ Tropical Beach — white sand with palm-adjacent flora
🏝️ Rainforest Island — jungle island rising from tropical sea
🌿 Tropical Wilds — open jungle savanna with scattered trees

---

**🌾 Tundra Region**
Boreal forests, magic groves, and highland terrain.

🌲 Tundra Taiga — classic boreal forest with spruce and fir
🍂 Autumn Tundra — orange-leaved boreal woodland
🌲 Redwood Forest — towering ancient sequoias
✨ Magic Forest — glowing teal/blue forest with unusual flora
🌿 Bonsai Forest — small twisted bonsai-like trees on rocky terrain
🏔️ Frosted Peaks — sharp icy summits above the boreal zone
🌊 Tundra River — wide cold river cutting through boreal terrain

---

**🌊 Swamp Region**
Dark wetlands, roofed forests, and murky waterways.

🌿 Swamp Forest — waterlogged woodland with lily pads
🐊 Mangrove Swamp — tangled mangrove roots in shallow water
🌳 Roofed Forest — dense dark oak canopy blocking sunlight
🌿 Willow Forest — drooping willow trees over marsh water
🌿 Marsh — shallow boggy wetland with tall grass
🌱 Cambian Drift — ancient mossy forest with unique terrain

---

**🔮 Terralost Region** *(rare — rarity 2, less common than other regions)*
Unique handcrafted biomes found nowhere else. The rarest terrain in the world.

💎 Amethyst Canyon — purple crystal formations in deep canyon walls
💜 Amethyst Rainforest — jungle overgrown with amethyst crystal growths
🏜️ Ancient Sands — ancient crumbling desert with buried ruins
🌿 Alpine Grove — high-altitude grove with unique alpine flora
🏔️ Alpine Highlands — exposed rocky alpine plateau

---

**🌊 Oceans (found between all regions)**
🌊 Deep Ocean — dark abyssal waters
🌊 Rich Ocean — coral and kelp-filled shallower waters
☀️ Warm Ocean — tropical shallow warm waters
🌊 Dark Depth Ocean — the deepest, darkest ocean layer

---

**🗿 Notable: Spawn Village is located in the Volcanic Plains biome at 0,0.**
All other regions generate beyond the volcanic zone — the further you explore, the more diverse the terrain becomes.
