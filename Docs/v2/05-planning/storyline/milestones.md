# Lost Wilderness V2 Storyline & Milestones

This document details the 7-chapter storyline progression and the percentage-based completion tracking system.

## 📊 Completion Percentage Calculation

The game tracks progress from 0% to 300% across three major tiers:

### 1. 0-100%: Story Milestones (The 7 Chapters)
*   **Nether Portal Entry:** 10%
*   **Ender Dragon Defeated:** 20%
*   **36 Wither Kills (Clan):** 30%
*   **6 Devoider Kills (Clan):** 40%
*   **El Diablo Defeated:** 50%
*   **Heavenly Tower Completed:** 70%
*   **Lord's Plateau Reached:** 100%

### 2. 100-200%: Vanilla Advancements
*   Each completed vanilla advancement (Story, Nether, End, Adventure, Husbandry) contributes to this tier.

### 3. 200-300%: Elemental Temples
*   Each of the 5 temples completed grants +20%:
    *   Fire Temple: 220%
    *   Earth Temple: 240%
    *   Wind Temple: 260%
    *   Water Temple: 280%
    *   Aether Temple: 300%

## 🗺️ The 7-Chapter Storyline (The "Holy" Path)

### Chapter 1: The First Flight
*   **Focus:** Survival on Earth and defeating the Ender Dragon.
*   **Unlock:** Reveals the **Corrupt Portal** (Crying Obsidian) on the next Easter.

### Chapter 2: Splitting the Firmament
*   **Focus:** Exploration of the Amplified world and defeating the **Father of Ender**.
*   **Unlock:** Reveals **Heaven's Gate** (Y=602 Aether portal).

### Chapter 3: The Withering Council
*   **Focus:** Clan-based effort to defeat **36 standard Withers**.
*   **Requirement:** Team/Clan effort only (no alliances).
*   **Unlock:** Permits entry to **The Devoid** dimension via the Normal Nether Roof.

### Chapter 4: Into the Void
*   **Focus:** Defeating **6 Devoiders** within the puzzle-based Devoid dimension.
*   **Unlock:** Permits entry to **Diablo’s Lair** on the Amplified Nether Roof.

### Chapter 5: The Father of Lies
*   **Focus:** Confronting **El Diablo** in his Bedrock Castle.
*   **Unlock:** Reveals the path to **Aquaria**.

### Chapter 6: Heavenly Trials
*   **Focus:** Defeating **Neptune** (Giant Drowned) and scaling the **3,000-block Heavenly Tower**.
*   **Requirements:** Answer Tower questions truthfully + Pass 5 "Good Fridays."
*   **Unlock:** Crossing the Rainbow Path to **Lord's Plateau**.

### Chapter 7: The Plateau of the Lord
*   **Focus:** Meeting The Lord face-to-face.
*   **Unlock:** Reveals the player's **Star Sign** and **Spirit Mob**, and unlocks the **Galactic Quest** (Mars through Pluto).

## 👥 Solo vs. Clan Implementation Notes
*   **Withering Council:** Currently designed as a mandatory team effort. For solo release, requirements may need scaling (e.g., 6 Withers for solo players vs 36 for clans).
*   **Reputation Impact:** Solo players not in clans may be treated as "Outlaws" or "Neutrals" by default until they choose a path.
