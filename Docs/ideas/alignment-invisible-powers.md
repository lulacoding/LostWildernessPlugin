# Alignment — Invisible Powers for Neutral Players

A design idea for how neutral (unaligned) players experience the world once good/evil powers are distributed to aligned players.


## Why This Works

- **Lore-justified:** Neutral players haven't committed to a cosmic side — they literally cannot perceive that layer of reality
- **Gameplay tension:** Neutral players are at a disadvantage in alignment-based PvP — they can't see what's coming or counter it
- **Incentive to align:** Makes choosing a side meaningful; the moment you commit, the veil lifts and you can perceive (and use) powers
- **Mystery for outsiders:** Server observers or new players who haven't aligned yet experience the world as strange and unexplained — adds atmosphere


## Alignment Tiers (Context)

There are three states:
| State | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Good / Light | Access to light-side abilities; perceive all alignment powers |
| Evil / Dark | Access to dark-side abilities; perceive all alignment powers |
| Neutral (normie) | No alignment powers; cannot perceive aligned combat |

> "Psychos, schizos, and normies. In Minecraft."

The game works and is completable in all three states — neutral is a valid playstyle, just deliberately blind to the supernatural layer of the world.
