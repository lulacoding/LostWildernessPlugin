# Gunsmithing & Firearms

A dedicated **Gunsmith** skill that gates weapon technology the same way Engineering gates vehicles. Starts in the black powder era and ends with alien weaponry.


## Alien Weapons (Tier 6)

Available after interacting with Zeratari technology. Examples:
- **Raygun** — energy beam; no bullet drop; alignment-coloured (light = white/gold, dark = purple/black)
- **Anti-gravity gun** — launches entities/blocks; utility weapon as much as combat
- **Recoilless rifle** — no knockback on the shooter; fires high-velocity slugs
- **Plasma rifle** — slow projectile, high AOE damage; alien manufacture only
- **Alignment variants** — light-side and dark-side versions of Zeratari weapons may behave differently (see [Matter engines and fuel tiers](matter-engines-and-fuel-tiers.md))


## Aiming System

**Preferred:** Iron sight / scope aim — if achievable in Paper, this would be ideal.

**Fallback (acceptable):** HUD crosshair acts as the gun's crosshair. The existing action bar / boss bar HUD (same system as vehicle sensors) can display weapon state (ammo, safety on/off, etc.).

Both options work for Java and Bedrock. Scope aim may require a resource pack zoom effect or custom FOV manipulation.


## Vehicle Integration

Gunsmiths + Engineers collaborate for:
- Vehicle-mounted cannons (flatbed truck + cannon schematic)
- SAM (surface-to-air missile) trailer — requires both gunsmith and engineering research
- Dual control vehicle + mounted turret: one drives, one shoots
