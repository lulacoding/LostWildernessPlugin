# Lost Wilderness: Honor & Alignment Scale

## The Full Honor Scale

The Honor Score is a dynamic sliding scale. Every player has a running score that changes based on their actions. The score determines the player's **dynamic title**, which updates as they cross thresholds.

### Story-Gated Tiers (Top & Bottom)

| Score | Title | Unlocked By |
|---|---|---|
| +50,000 | **God** | Reality Checkerboard completion (Chapter 8) |
| +25,000 | **Demigod** | Edge of Sol completion (Chapter 6) |

### Standard Tiers

| Score Range | Title |
|---|---|
| +5,000 | **Archangel** |
| +4,000 | **Angel** ← Chapter 4 Good requirement |
| +2,500 | **Hero** |
| +1,500 | **Noble** |
| +800 | **Honorable** |
| -200 to +200 | **Neutral** |
| -800 | **Villain** |
| -1,500 | **Wicked** |
| -2,500 | **Condemned** ← Chapter 4 Evil requirement |
| -4,000 | **Damned** ← Irreversible (El Diablo fork only) |
| -5,000 | **Demon** ← Branded at Void Altar (Evil Chapter 3) |

### Story-Gated Tiers (Bottom)

| Score | Title | Unlocked By |
|---|---|---|
| -25,000 | **Abyss Incarnate** | Equivalent of Edge of Sol (Evil path) |
| -50,000 | **The Devil** | Reality Checkerboard completion (Evil path, Chapter 8) |


## Honor Triggers by Alignment

### Good Player Triggers (Honor gain)
- Heal injured players
- Donate resources to new players
- Complete Celestial quests (temple runs, Kraft's scientific missions)
- Defend villages from raids without looting them
- Spare enemy players in PvP
- Fast on Good Friday
- Visit sacred structures and complete their trials

### Evil Player Triggers (Honor loss)
- Kill passive players unprovoked
- Poison water supplies
- Betray allies
- Commit war crimes during declared wars (no declaration, civilian casualties)
- Conduct Cyber War attacks
- Recruit for Orion or El Diablo during Zeratari campaign
- Perform dark Void Altar rituals

*Design principle: Both paths have equal depth. Good players never miss out on "fun" mechanics — they have a fully parallel suite of celestial abilities, channels, and story rewards.*
