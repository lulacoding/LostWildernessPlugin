# Lost Wilderness: The 7-Chapter Storyline

## Chapter 1: Restoring Cosmic Balance

**The First Boot & Arrival:**
- *Cutscene 1 (Pre-Lobby):* The player connects for the very first time. An unskippable, tile-based video cutscene plays full-screen before they even see the Lobby. (Content TBD).
- *The Awakening:* The player spawns in the Lobby, verifies their account, and chooses their initial traits.
- *Cutscene 2 (Survival Entry):* Stepping through the Gateway portal into the Survival world triggers a second cinematic, establishing their physical arrival on Earth. (Content TBD).

**The First Anomaly:**
The player begins standard survival — punching trees, mining, and gearing up. Nothing appears out of the ordinary. The first true divergence happens when they reach the End dimension. They do not face the Ender Dragon. They face **Mother Ender**. Defeating her is the first major narrative milestone.

**The First Wither & Professor Kraft:**
Returning to the Overworld, the player gathers wither skulls and summons their first Wither. Upon defeating it, a random world encounter triggers: a roaming NPC named **Professor Kraft** appears. He leaves them with a cryptic directive: *"Experiment with crying obsidian. When you have bridged the dimensions, we will speak again."*

**The Corrupt Portal (Dimensional Bridge):**
The player builds an Amplified Portal (Crying Obsidian frame ignited with flint and steel). Stepping through transports them to the **Amplified World** — a parallel Earth with impossibly extreme vertical terrain (Y=320).

**The Amplified Errand & Side Quests:**
Professor Kraft intercepts them in the Amplified World. He provides coordinates to his nearest lab and tasks them with retrieving RNG-selected items from the Amplified terrain. Between this information drop and the next, Kraft offers several **Side Quests** to pace the progression.

**Father of End:**
After delivering the items and completing side quests, Kraft directs the player to the **Amplified End** to slay the counterpart to Mother Ender: the **Father of End**.

*(End of Chapter 1)*


## Chapter 3: Ascending the Firmament

**The Aether Portal:**
The player must build a **Glowstone** portal frame and ignite it with a **Water Bucket**. This transports them to the start of the heavenly ascent.

**The Hike to Heaven (Aquaria to The Lord):**
There are no boss fights on this journey — it is a long, loot-filled pilgrimage through several layers:
1. **Aquaria** — A fully submerged world. Players can breathe underwater. They navigate an underwater empire filled with loot.
2. **Cascade Tower** — In the center of Aquaria. Players climb to its peak.
3. **The Rainbow Path** — At the top of Cascade Tower, the Rainbow Path begins: a high-speed, vertical ice-boat highway made of rainbow blocks that winds up through the clouds.
4. **Heaven's Tower** — A 3,000-block-tall tower containing only a spiral staircase.

**The Holy Inquisition:**
At the top of Heaven's Tower, **Gabriel** administers the **Holy Questionnaire** to each player individually. Passing the test opens the final cloud stairs to **The Lord's Plateau**.

*(End of Chapter 3)*


## Chapter 5: Ascending the Stars

**The Starport Unlocks:**
All three trials complete. The Lord grants access to further cloud stairs leading to the **Starport** — a celestial hub containing portals to all Solar System planets. Only **Venus** is initially active.

**Venus (The Baptism of Fire):**
The first space trial. Fire immunity (earned from maxed Personality/Skills) lets the player survive the extreme surface temperature. The player must survive the surface, gear up, and descend into the planet's core.

**The Cross-Path Core Bosses (Good vs. Evil):**
The Core Bosses of Venus and Mercury introduce a groundbreaking PvPvE mechanic. 
- *The Convergence:* While Good players travel here via The Lord's Starport, Evil players travel here using a **Demon Core** (a portable, single-use portal generator that destroys itself upon entry and is bound exclusively to its "master" player's UUID).
- *The Battle:* The Core boss fight is an instanced, synchronized matchmaking event. A Good party (or solo player) and an Evil party (or solo player) are thrown into the same boss arena simultaneously.
- *The Condition:* They must fight the boss *and* each other. To progress the story, a party must land the final blows or secure the victory condition against both the boss and the opposing alignment's party. If the Good party wins, the Evil party fails the instance and must re-queue to try again against a new Good party.

**Mercury:**
Venus progression naturally leads to Mercury. The player must repeat the survival and gearing process, culminating in another high-stakes PvPvE Core Boss fight against the opposing alignment.

**The Moon Port Unlocks:**
Having survived both planets, defeated the Core Bosses, and secured the Venus and Mercury exclusive skills/enchantments, the player returns to The Lord. He unlocks the Moon Port.

*(End of Chapter 5)*


## Chapter 7: The Andromeda Galaxy (Zeratari System)

**The Faint Signal:**
After Pluto, The Lord reveals a faint signature of El Diablo's corruption in the Andromeda Galaxy. The **Galaxy Port** opens beyond the Starport. First portal active: **Myrikane** (the dark mirror of Earth).

**NPC Alliance:**
- Solo players gain 1 NPC Ally.
- 8-player party gains 8 NPC Allies (total 16-unit skirmishes).

**The Andromeda Conquest:**
1. Velkorn (Scorched city-sprawl. Brute NPC Empires.)
2. Zeruun (Acid bubble-cities. Brute NPC Empires.)
3. Xarneth (Endless desert.)
4. Myrikane (Dark Earth mirror. Entry point. Good-war ally.)
5. Kyrothis (Amplified frozen peaks. Rebel faction fights FOR Orion.)
6. Tarnaxis (Underground canyon societies. Too hot on surface. Evil clans fight for Orion.)
7. Qirellon (Dense pine forests. Skinny Hivemind aliens. Orion's allies.)
8. Nytharix (Cyberpunk Republic. Good alignment. Triggering the Galactic War when Earth's clan joins.)
9. **Orion** (Authoritarian Cyberpunk Death Star. Allied with El Diablo.)

**The Destruction of Orion:**
The final mission: sabotage the planetary core. Orion is destroyed. Its ruins remain permanently on the server as a scavengeable post-apocalyptic wasteland.

**Return to the Lord's Plateau** — where the final siege begins.

*(End of Chapter 7)*


## The Epilogue: Ascension & Damnation (The Finale)

- **Good Path:** The Lord crowns the player **Archangel**. They receive celestial abilities and items.
- **Evil Path:** El Diablo's banishment formally registers the player to the Abyss as a **Demon**. They receive abyssal abilities and items.
- **The Ultimate Reward:** A cascade of custom achievements unlocks. BOTH Good and Evil players receive the exact same unique custom **Music Disc** to signify the end of the narrative.
- An endgame **Credits Cutscene** plays before returning the player to spawn.
- *100% Easter Egg:* All Vanilla advancements + all custom advancements completed = a second meme-y Easter Egg Music Disc.
- The player is now permanently immune to Void damage and begins the post-game sandbox as a demigod (or primeval terror).

*(End of Storyline)*
