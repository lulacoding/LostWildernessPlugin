# World Seeds & Chakra Progression

The multiverse is structured around chakra progression — each plane a player ascends to has increasingly dramatic terrain generation, from humble vanilla beginnings to violent Iris amplified worlds at the top.


## Seed Selection Philosophy

- The **spawn world (Root)** seed matters most — it sets the tone for the whole server
- He wants the spawn biome to stay as-is: a **jungle beach coast** leading to a warm ocean cove
- Deliberate decision: has NOT explored further than spawn to avoid ruining the surprise
- Deliberately avoiding looking up village locations, strongholds, etc. — wants the genuine discovery experience
- Other planet/dimension seeds matter less; they can be set freely


## Food Expiry & Appliance System (Idea)

Tied to the electricity/technology progression:

- **Food expiry** — food items spoil after a set time; encourages active food supply chains
- **Icebox / fridge** — early-game appliance that slows or stops food expiry
- **Full fridge** — later electric version, requires power infrastructure
- This system rewards players who invest in base infrastructure and punishes hoarding
- Meshes naturally with seasonal survival (see below)
