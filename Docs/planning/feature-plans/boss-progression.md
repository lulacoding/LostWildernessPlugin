# Lost Wilderness V2 Boss Progression & Requirements

This document outlines the official playthrough order for bosses, including requirements, unlocks, and level design concepts.

## Phase 1: The Vanilla Foundation
### 1. The Ender Dragon (Overworld/End)
*   **Requirement:** Standard vanilla progression.
*   **Unlock:** Reveals the **Corrupt Portal** (Crying Obsidian frames) to the player/clan.

### 2. The Father of Ender (Amplified End)
*   **Requirement:** Must have killed at least one Ender Dragon.
*   **Unlock:** Opens access to **Heaven's Gate** (Aether portal at Y=602 in Amplified).


## Phase 3: The Deeper Hell
### 5. El Diablo, Destroyer of Worlds
*   **Requirement:** Be involved in 6 Devoider kills.
*   **Level Design:** **Diablo’s Lair**. A Bedrock Castle with breakable obsidian segments, Wither Skeleton traps, and a Giant Wither Skeleton miniboss.
*   **The Fight:** 3-Phase battle ending with a Red Wither that destroys Bedrock.
*   **Unlock:** Reveals the path to **Aquaria** and the Celestial arc.


## Phase 5: The End of Reality
### 8. The Undoer (Pluto/Edge of Sol)
*   **Requirement:** Complete the Galactic Journey (Mars through Pluto Core).
*   **Unlock:** Triggers the move to Andromeda/Zeratari System.

### 9. Armageddon (Edge of the Universe)
*   **Requirement:** Reach Orion Core and confront the **False Flame**.
*   **Level Design:** The **Chess Board Arena**. Players are Pawns fighting alongside The Lord against the combined forces of El Diablo (Destroyer of Reality), the Witch of Antimatter, and The Undoer.

## Branching Logic: Pledging to Darkness
*   **The Pledge:** Using a **Wither Rose** on a boss to "Pledge to the Darkness."
*   **Mechanic:** Requirements flip from "Defeat" to "Sacrifice/Corrupt." Progression leads to a deeper, more hostile Nether instead of Heaven.
