# Lost Wilderness V2 Storyline & Milestones

This document details the 7-chapter storyline progression and the percentage-based completion tracking system.

## 📊 Completion Percentage Calculation

The game tracks progress from 0% to 300% across three major tiers:

### 1. 0-100%: Story Milestones (The 7 Chapters)
*   **Nether Portal Entry:** 10%
*   **Ender Dragon Defeated:** 20%
*   **36 Wither Kills (Clan):** 30%
*   **6 Devoider Kills (Clan):** 40%
*   **El Diablo Defeated:** 50%
*   **Heavenly Tower Completed:** 70%
*   **Lord's Plateau Reached:** 100%

### 2. 100-200%: Vanilla Advancements
*   Each completed vanilla advancement (Story, Nether, End, Adventure, Husbandry) contributes to this tier.

### 3. 200-300%: Elemental Temples
*   Each of the 5 temples completed grants +20%:
    *   Fire Temple: 220%
    *   Earth Temple: 240%
    *   Wind Temple: 260%
    *   Water Temple: 280%
    *   Aether Temple: 300%


## 👥 Solo vs. Clan Implementation Notes
*   **Withering Council:** Currently designed as a mandatory team effort. For solo release, requirements may need scaling (e.g., 6 Withers for solo players vs 36 for clans).
*   **Reputation Impact:** Solo players not in clans may be treated as "Outlaws" or "Neutrals" by default until they choose a path.
